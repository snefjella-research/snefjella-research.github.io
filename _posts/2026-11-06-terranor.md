## How Terranor makes money

Terranor operates and maintains roads for public clients in Sweden, Finland and Denmark. The work covers winter operations (snow clearing, gritting) and summer maintenance (repairs, asphalt, drainage), plus green-space maintenance, road safety and light infrastructure projects. Most revenue comes from multi-year contracts won in public tenders, mainly from national road agencies and municipalities.

The business was carved out of NCC. Mutares bought the Swedish and Finnish operations in 2020 and the Danish business in 2021, listed the company on Nasdaq First North in June 2025, and sold its last shares in May 2026.
