## What happened: the Silicones transaction

In February 2026 Elkem agreed to sell the majority of its Silicones division to Bluestar, its largest shareholder and part of China's state-owned chemicals group. No cash changed hands. Instead, Elkem redeemed all 338,338,536 Elkem shares held by Bluestar, equal to 52.9% of the company. The deal was approved at an extraordinary general meeting on 9 March 2026 and completed on 30 April 2026. DNB Carnegie gave a fairness opinion to the independent board.

Elkem kept three Silicones assets outside the deal: Yongdeng (silicon metal in China), Roussillon (upstream silicones in France, which supplies Bluestar under a five-year agreement) and Chakan (downstream silicones in India). After closing, Elkem raised NOK 1.8 billion in new equity and refinanced EUR 1 billion of bank facilities.

The remaining Elkem now reports three divisions: **Elkem Silicon**, **Elkem Foundry Alloys** and **Elkem Carbon**. All shares are held by public investors.

## The cycle and trade protection

Silicon and ferrosilicon prices are weak in 2026. Management expects a gradual normalisation. The EU has renewed anti-dumping duties on ferrosilicon for five years (31.2% for Chinese and 22.7% for Russian producers) and opened a review of the 16.8% duty on Chinese silicon, expected to conclude within 12–15 months. EU safeguard measures took effect from 1 July 2026.
