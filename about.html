About

Why this site exists

I became interested in valuation and financial analysis during my studies. With part-time jobs alongside the degree, I never made time for equity research of my own, even though I had wanted to for a long time.

In interviews I have more than once been told that I lack a demonstrable interest in equities. Fair enough. So I decided to make the time, and this site is the result.

I am a finance MSc candidate at BI Norwegian Business School. My background is in valuation, financial modelling and transaction work, with audit experience before that.

What you will find here

  • Research notes on Nordic listed companies, one at a time.
  • The spreadsheets behind the notes, so the numbers can be checked and reused.
  • Articles and books I have found useful along the way.

If any of it makes the path a little easier for someone earlier in their studies, even better.
