About

Why this site exists

I became interested in valuation and financial analysis during my studies. With part-time jobs alongside the degree, I never made time for equity research of my own, even though I had wanted to for a long time.

In interviews I have more than once been told that I lack a demonstrable interest in equities. Fair enough. So I decided to make the time, and this site is the result.

I am a finance MSc candidate at BI Norwegian Business School. My background is in deal sourcing, financial modelling, accounting and audit.

What you will find here

  • Write-ups on Nordic listed companies, one at a time.
  • The spreadsheets behind the write-ups, so the numbers can be checked and reused.
  • Articles and books I have found useful along the way.

If any of it makes the path a little easier for someone earlier in their studies, even better.

Master thesis · BI · 2026

Operational performance in Nordic private equity buyouts

Do PE buyouts improve how Nordic companies operate? 226 buyouts from 1999 to 2023, tested with matched difference-in-differences. Revenue grows faster after a buyout, margins fall, and add-on platforms grow without the margin hit.

With Kristian Gjestrud · Supervisor: Janis Berzins

Equity research report on the Norwegian salmon farmer after its restructuring, built on a DCF supported by relative valuation. Buy recommendation with a target of NOK 80.84 against NOK 67.90 on 31 October 2025, a 19% upside.

Bachelor thesis · BI Bergen · 2023 · In Norwegian

Valuation of SalMar ASA

Fundamental valuation of the salmon farmer as of 31 December 2021: DCF at a 7.07% WACC, peer multiples and a Monte Carlo simulation. Fair value NOK 512 per share against a market price of NOK 607, a 16% downside.
