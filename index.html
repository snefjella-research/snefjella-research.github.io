Road operation & maintenance

Terranor: will the margins follow the growth?

A newly listed Nordic road-maintenance specialist, carved out of NCC and turned around under Mutares. Sweden has raised its 2026–2037 road budget by 48%, and Terranor won 31% of the latest Swedish tender round.

Growth is not the hard part. Margins are. The case rests on whether the company can deliver the margin expansion it guided for 2026–2028, and how much of reported earnings turns into cash.
