Elkem: what is left after Silicones?

In April 2026 Elkem handed most of its Silicones division to its Chinese main owner, Bluestar, in exchange for Bluestar's 52.9% stake. No cash changed hands. The listed company is now owned entirely by minority investors.

What remains is a European producer of silicon, ferroalloys and carbon products, with heavy exposure to Chinese overcapacity and new EU trade measures. The write-up will test whether the simpler Elkem is worth more than the market thinks.
