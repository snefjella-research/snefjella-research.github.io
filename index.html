Road operation & maintenance · Listed Jun 2025

Terranor: will the margins follow the growth?

A newly listed Nordic road-maintenance specialist, carved out of NCC and turned around under Mutares. The structural backdrop is strong. Sweden raised its 2026–2037 road budget by 48%, and Terranor won 31% of the latest Swedish tender round, lifting the order book to record levels.

Growth is not really the question here. Margins are. The business has long run thin, and the whole case rests on whether it can deliver the margin expansion it guided for 2026–2028.
